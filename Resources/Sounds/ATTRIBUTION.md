Alarm sounds bundled in this directory are generated locally for this app.

- `gentle-morning.caf`: soft ascending morning chime.
- `forest-bird.caf`: light high chime pattern.
- `ocean-breeze.caf`: calmer low two-note chime.
- `classic-bell.caf`: clean double-bell motif.

Files are under 30 seconds and encoded as CAF for iOS local notification and in-app playback compatibility.
